## Mentor Playbook

### Role Purpose
Mentors guide participants through pilot execution, verify credentials for protected activities, create opportunities for entrepreneurs and students, and provide expert validation and endorsements.

### Journey Phases

**1. Registration** (Entry Phase)
- **Required Fields**: name, email, availability, consent
- **Unlocks**: View pilot listings, browse mentor resources
- **Criteria**: Complete basic profile, accept terms and conditions

**2. Credential Verification**
- **Required Fields**: certifications, jurisdictions served, specializations
- **Unlocks**: Apply to mentor pilots, access verification tools, view pending reviews
- **Criteria**: Upload at least one certification, pass license verification, define service area

**3. Active Mentoring**
- **Required Fields**: max mentees per month, hourly rate
- **Unlocks**: Accept mentee requests, conduct reviews, earn reputation points
- **Criteria**: Complete 5+ mentoring sessions, maintain 4.5+ rating, no compliance violations

**4. Trusted Advisor** (Advanced Phase)
- **Unlocks**: Priority matching, featured mentor badge, advanced analytics, mentor other mentors
- **Criteria**: Complete 25+ mentoring sessions, maintain 4.8+ rating, active for 6+ months

### What Mentors Can Do

#### Primary Capabilities
- **Review Pilots**: Evaluate pilot submissions for feasibility, compliance, and quality
- **Verify Licenses**: Run verification checks on participant credentials and certifications
- **Create Opportunities**: Post new opportunities for entrepreneurs and students to apply
- **Provide Guidance**: Offer private mentorship notes and public endorsements
- **Gate Protected Activities**: Ensure protected activities have appropriate licensed oversight

#### Management Actions
- **Dashboard Access**: View pending verifications, active pilots, opportunities
- **Filter & Search**: Filter pilots by status (pending, under review, approved, all)
- **Opportunity Management**: Create, edit, publish, close, or delete opportunities
- **Review Queue**: Prioritize and process verification requests

#### Available Actions

**On Pilots:**
- View pilot details and evidence bundles
- Preview evidence attachments
- Download evidence bundles
- Run license verification checks
- Request clarification from pilot owner
- Approve non-protected pilots
- Flag issues for admin review
- Add private reviewer notes
- Write public endorsements

**On Opportunities:**
- Create new opportunity (draft)
- Edit opportunity details
- Publish opportunity (make visible to users)
- View matched participants
- Close opportunity (stop accepting applicants)
- Delete opportunity (remove from system)

**On Participants:**
- Review participant fit scores
- Send mentorship invitations
- Schedule office hours
- Provide feedback and ratings

### Typical Workflows

#### Workflow 1: Review a Pilot Submission
1. Log into Mentor Dashboard
2. Check "Pending Verifications" count in stats
3. Switch to "Pending" tab to see pilots awaiting review
4. Click on a pilot card to open details
5. Click "Preview Evidence" to inspect attachments
6. If protected activities are flagged:
   - Click "Verify License" to open verification panel
   - Enter or confirm registry ID
   - Click "Verify" and wait for result
   - Review verification status (verified/expired/not_found)
7. If non-protected or verified:
   - Add reviewer note if needed
   - Approve pilot or request clarification
8. If issues found:
   - Flag for admin review
   - Add detailed notes about concerns

#### Workflow 2: Create an Opportunity
1. Navigate to Mentor Dashboard
2. Click "+ Create Opportunity" button
3. Fill opportunity form:
   - Title and description
   - Required skills and certifications
   - Minimum experience years
   - Jurisdictions where opportunity is available
   - Availability requirements
   - Compensation and duration
   - Max participants
4. Add protected activity flags if applicable
5. Save as draft or publish immediately
6. Monitor applications as participants apply

#### Workflow 3: Provide Mentorship
1. Review assigned mentee requests
2. Accept mentee pairing
3. Schedule office hours via calendar link
4. Access pilot details for mentee's work
5. Add private mentorship notes
6. Track mentee progress
7. Provide feedback and ratings
8. Write endorsement upon successful completion

### Key Metrics & KPIs
- Pending verifications count
- Active pilots under review
- Published opportunities count
- Mentee satisfaction rating
- Review completion time
- Endorsements given

## Student Playbook

### Role Purpose
Students learn by participating in pilots, building portfolios, and developing skills through supervised project work. They progress through phases that unlock more advanced opportunities.

### Journey Phases

**1. Onboarding** (Entry Phase)
- **Required Fields**: name, email, availability, consent
- **Unlocks**: Browse pilot opportunities, access learning resources
- **Criteria**: Complete basic profile, accept terms and conditions

**2. Skill Building**
- **Required Fields**: skills, experience years, device access, internet reliability
- **Unlocks**: Get matched to pilots, request mentor pairing, join learning groups
- **Criteria**: Add at least 3 skills, specify device access, confirm internet reliability

**3. Portfolio Building**
- **Required Fields**: willing to upskill, willing to partner with licensed professionals
- **Unlocks**: Apply to protected pilots (with mentor), track portfolio progress, request recommendations
- **Criteria**: Complete 3+ pilot contributions, receive mentor feedback, build verified portfolio

**4. Pilot Ready** (Advanced Phase)
- **Optional Fields**: certifications
- **Unlocks**: Lead pilot sections, mentor newer students, priority matching, certification fast-track
- **Criteria**: Complete 10+ pilot contributions, average 4.5+ rating, optional certification

### What Students Can Do

#### Primary Capabilities
- **Browse Opportunities**: Discover published opportunities matching their skills
- **Submit Pilots**: Create and submit pilot proposals (with mentor for protected activities)
- **Build Portfolio**: Track contributions and collect recommendations
- **Request Mentorship**: Apply for mentor pairing and guidance
- **Upskill**: Access learning resources and training materials

#### Management Actions
- **Profile Completion**: Progressive profile updates as phases unlock
- **Match Tracking**: View opportunity match scores and fit levels
- **Progress Monitoring**: Track completion percentage and next unlocks
- **Task Management**: Review assigned tasks and annotation work

#### Available Actions

**On Opportunities:**
- Browse published opportunities
- View opportunity details and requirements
- Check match score and fit level
- Review matched/missing requirements
- Apply to opportunities (if qualified)

**On Pilots:**
- Create pilot drafts
- Upload evidence (non-protected)
- Request mentor for protected activities
- Submit pilot for review
- Track pilot status
- View feedback and ratings

**On Profile:**
- Complete profile fields for current phase
- Add skills and certifications
- Update availability and preferences
- Upload portfolio work
- Request mentor recommendations

**On Learning:**
- Access tutorials and guides
- Join learning groups
- Participate in annotation tasks
- Complete training modules
- Earn phase completion badges

### Typical Workflows

#### Workflow 1: Apply to an Opportunity
1. Log into Student Dashboard
2. Click "Browse Opportunities"
3. Review list of published opportunities
4. Click on opportunity card to view details
5. Review match score and fit analysis
6. Check matched vs. missing requirements
7. If qualified, click "Apply"
8. Wait for mentor review/matching
9. Receive notification of acceptance

#### Workflow 2: Submit a Pilot
1. Navigate to "Submit New Pilot"
2. Fill pilot form:
   - Title and description
   - Jurisdictions
   - Expected KPIs
   - Check any protected activity flags
3. If protected activities flagged:
   - Add partner fallback note, or
   - Upload evidence from licensed partner, or
   - Request mentor assignment
4. Upload supporting evidence files
5. Submit pilot for review
6. Track status in dashboard
7. Respond to clarification requests
8. Receive approval/rejection notification

#### Workflow 3: Progress Through Phases
1. Complete onboarding profile fields
2. Navigate to "Complete Profile" prompt
3. Fill phase-specific required fields
4. Add optional fields for bonus features
5. Submit profile update
6. Review new unlocks and capabilities
7. Access newly available features
8. Work toward next phase criteria
9. Receive notification when eligible for advancement

### Key Metrics & KPIs
- Profile completion percentage
- Current phase and next unlock
- Pilot contributions count
- Match score average
- Portfolio items completed
- Mentor recommendations received

## Entrepreneur Playbook

### Role Purpose
Entrepreneurs create and execute pilots, manage projects and teams, track KPIs, and scale successful business ideas. They partner with licensed professionals for protected activities and build portfolios of validated work.

### Journey Phases

**1. Idea Stage** (Entry Phase)
- **Required Fields**: name, email, consent
- **Unlocks**: Browse platform capabilities, access entrepreneur resources, view sample pilots
- **Criteria**: Complete basic profile, accept terms and conditions

**2. Planning**
- **Required Fields**: availability, business name, business description, target market
- **Unlocks**: Create draft pilots, use pilot planning tools, invite collaborators
- **Criteria**: Define business context, create first pilot draft, identify compliance requirements

**3. Active Pilot**
- **Required Fields**: skills, jurisdictions served
- **Unlocks**: Submit pilots for review, recruit team members, track KPIs, access audit tools
- **Criteria**: Have 1+ approved pilot, build pilot team, demonstrate KPI tracking

**4. Scaling** (Advanced Phase)
- **Unlocks**: Multi-pilot management, advanced analytics, API access, priority support
- **Criteria**: Complete 3+ successful pilots, maintain compliance record, demonstrate scaling metrics

### What Entrepreneurs Can Do

#### Primary Capabilities
- **Create Pilots**: Design and submit pilot projects
- **Manage Teams**: Recruit and coordinate licensed partners and contributors
- **Track KPIs**: Monitor project metrics and performance
- **Browse Opportunities**: Find opportunities to participate in as contributor
- **Build Portfolio**: Showcase successful pilot executions
- **Scale Projects**: Manage multiple concurrent pilots

#### Management Actions
- **Dashboard Access**: View project portfolio, KPIs, fit profile
- **Project Management**: Create, edit, track pilot status
- **Team Coordination**: Invite partners, assign roles, manage access
- **Evidence Management**: Upload, preview, download evidence bundles
- **Compliance Tracking**: Monitor protected activities and requirements

#### Available Actions

**On Pilots:**
- Create new pilot (draft)
- Edit pilot details
- Add jurisdictions and activity flags
- Upload evidence files
- Add partner fallback notes
- Submit for review
- Track pilot status
- View reviewer feedback
- Download evidence bundles
- Close or archive pilots

**On Teams:**
- Invite licensed partners
- Assign mentors to protected activities
- Invite collaborators
- Manage team member access
- Track team member contributions

**On Opportunities:**
- Browse published opportunities
- View match scores
- Apply as participant/contributor
- Track application status

**On Analytics:**
- View KPI dashboard
- Track pilot performance metrics
- Review fit profile and score
- Monitor compliance status
- Export project reports

### Typical Workflows

#### Workflow 1: Launch a New Pilot
1. Log into Entrepreneur Dashboard
2. Click "Submit New Pilot"
3. Fill pilot form:
   - Title and clear description
   - Target jurisdictions
   - Expected KPIs and success metrics
   - Check protected activity flags if applicable
4. If protected activities are involved:
   - Option A: Add partner fallback note explaining licensed oversight
   - Option B: Upload evidence from licensed partner
   - Option C: Invite licensed mentor to project
5. Upload supporting evidence and documentation
6. Review fit badge and match result
7. Submit pilot for review
8. Monitor "Under Review" status
9. Respond to clarification requests
10. Receive approval and begin execution

#### Workflow 2: Manage Active Project
1. Navigate to Entrepreneur Dashboard
2. Review KPI summary cards
3. Click on pilot card to view details
4. Check current status and fit badge
5. Preview attached evidence
6. Review protected activity flags and partner notes
7. Add new evidence as project progresses
8. Invite additional team members if needed
9. Track progress toward KPI targets
10. Download evidence bundle for reporting

#### Workflow 3: Find and Join Opportunities
1. Click "Browse Opportunities" in dashboard
2. Review list of published opportunities
3. Filter by skills, compensation, duration
4. Click opportunity to view details
5. Review match score and fit analysis
6. Check requirements (matched vs. missing)
7. If interested and qualified, apply
8. Wait for mentor/opportunity owner response
9. Accept assignment if selected
10. Contribute to opportunity as team member

### Key Metrics & KPIs
- Active pilots count
- Completion rate percentage
- Average fit score
- Protected activities with verified partners
- Team members recruited
- KPI achievement rate

## Admin Playbook

### Role Purpose
Administrators oversee platform operations, audit activities, manage users, generate compliance reports, verify licenses, and ensure system integrity. They progress through access levels with increasing permissions.

### Journey Phases

**1. Observer** (Entry Phase)
- **Required Fields**: name, email, consent
- **Unlocks**: View system metrics, read-only audit access, browse all profiles
- **Criteria**: Complete admin onboarding, pass security training

**2. Reviewer**
- **Required Fields**: specializations, timezone
- **Unlocks**: Review pilot submissions, verify licenses, add reviewer notes, flag issues
- **Criteria**: Complete 20+ reviews, no escalated complaints, pass advanced compliance training

**3. Full Admin** (Advanced Phase)
- **Unlocks**: User management, system configuration, export all data, override decisions, manage other admins
- **Criteria**: Appointed by platform owner, complete all certifications

### What Admins Can Do

#### Primary Capabilities
- **Audit Activities**: Review all platform actions via audit timeline
- **Manage Users**: Create, edit, suspend, or delete user accounts
- **Verify Credentials**: Run comprehensive license and certification checks
- **Generate Reports**: Export audit logs, compliance reports, analytics
- **System Configuration**: Manage settings, permissions, integrations
- **Override Decisions**: Escalate or reverse decisions when needed

#### Management Actions
- **Dashboard Access**: Platform-wide statistics and activity overview
- **Audit Timeline**: Filter and search all logged actions
- **User Management**: View, edit, create user accounts and roles
- **License Verification**: Access comprehensive verification tools
- **Report Generation**: Export data in CSV, JSON formats

#### Available Actions

**On Audits:**
- View audit timeline
- Filter by user, pilot, date, action type
- Search audit logs
- Export audit data (CSV/JSON)
- View request IDs and timestamps
- Track evidence file references
- Review decision provenance

**On Users:**
- View all user profiles
- Filter by role, phase, status
- Create new user accounts
- Edit user information
- Suspend user access
- Delete user accounts
- Reset passwords
- Assign roles and permissions
- Override phase gates (with justification)

**On Pilots:**
- View all pilots (all statuses)
- Review pilot details and evidence
- Run license verifications
- Approve or reject pilots
- Request clarification
- Flag compliance issues
- Override mentor decisions
- Close or archive pilots
- Export pilot data

**On System:**
- Configure platform settings
- Manage partner program integrations
- Set verification thresholds
- Configure audit retention
- Manage API keys and webhooks
- Configure notification rules
- Export system logs

### Typical Workflows

#### Workflow 1: Review Audit Timeline
1. Log into Admin Dashboard
2. Review platform stats (users, pilots, protected activities)
3. Click "View Audit Timeline"
4. Apply filters:
   - By user (dropdown or search)
   - By pilot ID
   - By date range
   - By action type (create, verify, approve, etc.)
5. Review filtered audit entries
6. Click on entry to see details
7. Note requestId and timestamp
8. Export filtered audit data:
   - Click "Export CSV" or "Export JSON"
   - Save file for compliance reporting
9. Review evidence file references in export

#### Workflow 2: Verify User Credentials
1. Navigate to user profile or pilot detail
2. Click "Verify License" button
3. Review certification details:
   - Issuer name
   - Registry ID
   - Issue and expiration dates
4. Confirm registry endpoint
5. Click "Verify" to run check
6. Review verification result:
   - ✓ Verified: License is active and valid
   - ⚠ Expired: License has passed expiration date
   - ✗ Not Found: Registry ID not found
7. Add admin notes about verification
8. Decision based on result:
   - If verified: approve protected activity
   - If expired: request renewal evidence
   - If not found: flag for review or fallback
9. Verification logged to audit timeline

#### Workflow 3: Manage User Account
1. Navigate to Admin Dashboard
2. Click "User Management" or browse profiles
3. Search or filter to find target user
4. Click on user to view full profile
5. Review user details, phase, and activity
6. Take action as needed:
   - Edit profile information
   - Adjust phase or permissions
   - Suspend access (with reason)
   - Delete account (with confirmation)
   - Reset password
   - Send notification to user
7. Add admin note explaining action
8. Confirm changes
9. Action logged to audit timeline

### Key Metrics & KPIs
- Total users by role
- Total pilots by status
- Protected activities count
- Verification completion rate
- Average review time
- Compliance issues flagged
- Audit export frequency

## Analyst Playbook

### Role Purpose
Analysts inspect evidence, run verifications, produce compliance findings, export audit packages, and provide detailed forensic review of submissions. They bridge review and audit functions.

### Journey Phases

Analysts typically follow admin-like progression but with focus on evidence inspection:

**1. Evidence Observer**
- **Unlocks**: View evidence bundles, read-only access to pilots
- **Criteria**: Pass evidence handling training

**2. Verification Analyst**
- **Unlocks**: Run verifications, add evidence notes, flag inconsistencies
- **Criteria**: Complete 20+ evidence reviews

**3. Senior Analyst**
- **Unlocks**: Assign reviews to others, export compliance packages, override flags
- **Criteria**: 6+ months experience, advanced certification

### What Analysts Can Do

#### Primary Capabilities
- **Inspect Evidence**: Detailed review of uploaded evidence files and metadata
- **Run Verifications**: Execute license and certification verification checks
- **Flag Issues**: Identify inconsistencies, missing data, or compliance concerns
- **Export Reports**: Generate audit packages for compliance and reporting
- **Assign Reviews**: Route evidence to appropriate reviewers
- **Add Annotations**: Attach notes and findings to evidence items

#### Management Actions
- **Evidence Queues**: View and manage evidence review queues
  - New Evidence: Recently uploaded, not yet reviewed
  - Needs Review: Flagged for detailed inspection
  - Flagged: Issues identified, awaiting resolution
- **Search & Filter**: Find evidence by pilot, user, date, or status
- **Batch Operations**: Process multiple evidence items
- **Report Generation**: Export findings and audit data

#### Available Actions

**On Evidence:**
- Preview evidence files
- Check file metadata (hash, size, upload date)
- Verify signed URLs
- Add evidence notes
- Flag inconsistencies
- Mark as reviewed
- Request additional documentation
- Download evidence files
- Export evidence bundle

**On Pilots:**
- Browse all pilots
- Filter by jurisdiction, status, protected activities
- Open pilot detail
- View evidence summary
- Run comprehensive verifications
- Add analyst notes
- Assign to mentor or admin
- Flag for stakeholder follow-up

**On Verifications:**
- Access license verification panel
- Enter or verify registry IDs
- Run verification checks
- Review verification history
- Document verification outcomes
- Record partner fallback justifications
- Flag expired or invalid credentials

**On Reports:**
- Export audit slices by:
  - Date range
  - User or pilot
  - Verification status
  - Protected activity type
- Generate compliance reports
- Create evidence packages
- Export in CSV or JSON format
- Include checksums and provenance

### Typical Workflows

#### Workflow 1: Inspect Evidence Bundle
1. Log into Analyst Dashboard
2. Review evidence queues dashboard
3. Click on "New Evidence" queue
4. Select a pilot with new evidence
5. Click "Preview Evidence" to open viewer
6. For each evidence item:
   - View file metadata
   - Check checksum
   - Verify signed URL is accessible
   - Review issuer and registry ID
   - Note issue and expiration dates
7. If issues found:
   - Add evidence note detailing concern
   - Flag inconsistency
   - Record recommended action
8. If evidence is complete and valid:
   - Mark as reviewed
   - Add approval note
9. Move to next evidence item

#### Workflow 2: Run Verification and Record Outcome
1. Open pilot detail with protected activities
2. Navigate to evidence requiring verification
3. Click "Verify License" to open panel
4. Review pre-filled verification details:
   - Registry ID
   - Issuer
   - Issue/expiration dates
5. Confirm or adjust registry endpoint
6. Click "Verify" and wait for result
7. Document outcome:
   - **If Verified**: Record successful verification, proceed
   - **If Expired**: Add note requesting renewal, flag for follow-up
   - **If Not Found**: Record registry check failure
     - Check for partner fallback note
     - If fallback exists: document and recommend acceptance
     - If no fallback: flag for admin review
8. Add detailed analyst notes
9. Assign next action (approve, request clarification, escalate)

#### Workflow 3: Assign and Export Audit Report
1. Navigate to Analyst Dashboard
2. Identify pilot requiring review assignment
3. Review evidence and verification status
4. Determine appropriate reviewer:
   - If verified and complete: assign to mentor for approval
   - If issues flagged: assign to admin for investigation
   - If complex compliance: assign to senior analyst
5. Click "Assign to Reviewer"
6. Select reviewer from dropdown
7. Add assignment note with context
8. Confirm assignment
9. Generate audit export:
   - Click "Export Report"
   - Select format (CSV or JSON)
   - Include checksums and timestamps
   - Download and verify file
10. Confirm assignment appears in recipient's queue

### Typical Workflows (Additional)

#### Workflow 4: Process Evidence Queue
1. Navigate to "Needs Review" queue
2. Sort by priority (age, protected activity level)
3. Select batch of similar evidence types
4. For each item in batch:
   - Quick metadata check
   - Flag obvious issues
   - Mark compliant items as reviewed
5. Export batch review summary
6. Route flagged items to appropriate reviewers
7. Update queue status
8. Log batch processing completion

### Key Metrics & KPIs
- Evidence items reviewed
- Verifications completed
- Issues flagged
- Average review time per evidence item
- Assignment accuracy (% not returned)
- Reports exported
- Queue processing velocity

## Common Questions & Answers

### For Mentors

**Q: How do I handle a pilot with expired license evidence?**
A: Run verification, note the expiration, request updated evidence from pilot owner, and add reviewer note. Do not approve protected activities with expired licenses without explicit admin override.

**Q: Can I edit an opportunity after publishing?**
A: Yes, use the "Edit" action on published opportunities. Changes are tracked in audit log. Participants are notified of material changes.

**Q: What if I'm not qualified to review a protected activity?**
A: Flag the pilot for admin review with a note explaining the required expertise. Admin will reassign to a qualified reviewer.

### For Students

**Q: Why can't I apply to certain opportunities?**
A: Opportunities may require specific phase completion, certifications, experience levels, or availability. Check match score and missing requirements. Consider advancing your phase or adding required skills.

**Q: How do I get a mentor assigned?**
A: Request mentor pairing in your dashboard or when submitting a pilot with protected activities. System matches based on mentor specialization and availability.

**Q: Can I work on protected activities?**
A: Yes, but only with licensed mentor oversight or partner fallback arrangement. System enforces this requirement during pilot submission.

### For Entrepreneurs

**Q: What is a partner fallback note?**
A: A documented arrangement with a licensed professional who will oversee protected activities. Include their name, credentials, and role in the note field when submitting your pilot.

**Q: How do I track multiple pilots?**
A: Use the Entrepreneur Dashboard to view all pilots in card view. Filter by status and use color-coded fit badges to prioritize. Export pilot data for external tracking if needed.

**Q: Can I invite non-licensed collaborators?**
A: Yes, for non-protected activities. For protected activities, at least one team member must have verified credentials or you must document partner fallback.

### For Admins

**Q: When should I override a mentor decision?**
A: Only with clear justification and documentation. Typical cases: incorrect interpretation of policy, new regulatory guidance, or evidence of bias. All overrides are logged.

**Q: How long are audit logs retained?**
A: Check system configuration. Default is typically 7 years for compliance. Exports are immutable and should be archived externally for long-term retention.

**Q: Can I delete audit entries?**
A: No, audit log is immutable. Entries can be annotated with corrections or context, but not deleted. This ensures compliance and forensic integrity.

### For Analysts

**Q: What if verification registry is unavailable?**
A: Document the registry timeout/error, check for recent successful verifications of same registry, add note about technical issue, and either retry later or escalate to admin for manual verification if urgent.

**Q: How detailed should evidence notes be?**
A: Include: what you checked, what you found, any discrepancies, recommended action, and your analyst ID. Assume notes may be reviewed in compliance audit.

**Q: Can I approve pilots?**
A: Not directly. Analysts inspect and document, then assign to appropriate approver (mentor or admin). Senior analysts may have approval authority based on system configuration.

## Glossary

- **Fit Badge**: Visual indicator (High/Medium/Low) of how well a participant matches opportunity or pilot requirements
- **Protected Activity**: Task or operation requiring licensed professional oversight due to regulatory requirements
- **Partner Fallback**: Documented arrangement with licensed professional for protected activity oversight
- **Phase Gate**: Milestone requiring specific criteria before advancing to next journey phase
- **Evidence Bundle**: Collection of uploaded files, metadata, and verification records for a pilot
- **Registry Verification**: Automated check of credential validity against authoritative registry
- **Audit Timeline**: Immutable log of all system actions with timestamps and user attribution
