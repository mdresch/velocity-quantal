## Epics

1. Opportunity Discovery
   - Stories: E-01 (Curated Opportunity List)
   - Goal: Help entrepreneurs find and bookmark validated opportunities quickly.

2. Pilot Execution
   - Stories: E-02 (Start Pilot), PO-01 (Pilot Owner Management)
   - Goal: Enable rapid pilot creation, milestone tracking, evidence capture, and team coordination.

3. Mentorship & Validation
   - Stories: M-01 (Mentor Review & Annotations)
   - Goal: Provide mentor workflows for review, annotation, and milestone sign-off.

4. Management & Monitoring
   - Stories: PM-01 (Cohort Dashboard)
   - Goal: Provide PMs with cohort-level visibility, health indicators and escalation paths.

5. Admin & Governance
   - Stories: AD-01 (Invite & Role Assignment), AD-02 (Template Management)
   - Goal: Allow admins to onboard users, assign roles, manage templates, and audit activity.

6. Insights & Analysis
   - Stories: A-01 (Market Analyst Filters & Export)
   - Goal: Enable analysts to filter, prioritize, and export opportunities for decision-making.

## Flow (user-centric)

- Discover: Opportunity List → Opportunity Detail → Bookmark
- Act: Start Pilot → Configure Team & Milestones → Execute → Upload Evidence
- Validate: Mentor Review → Milestone Sign-Off → PM Dashboard Monitoring
- Govern: Admin invites users & manages templates → Audit & Reporting
